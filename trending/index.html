Buzzword of the Day

BUZZWORD OF THE DAY · NOV 28

Black Friday

The money word everyone's using, explained in 30 seconds.

What it means: The Friday after Thanksgiving, when U.S. retailers offer big discounts to kick off the holiday shopping season. It is one of the busiest shopping days of the year, both in stores and online.

Why it's buzzing: Adobe data cited by Reuters showed U.S. shoppers spent $9.7 billion online on Black Friday through 6:30 p.m. ET, up 12.8% from last year. (Reuters)
