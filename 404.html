BUZZWORD OF THE DAY · NOV 26

PCE price index

The money word everyone's using, explained in 30 seconds.

What it means: A government measure of how the prices people in the U.S. pay for goods and services are changing, used to track inflation. It is the inflation gauge the Federal Reserve prefers.

Why it's buzzing: The Commerce Department reported Wednesday that the PCE price index rose 0.3% in October and was up 3.8% from a year earlier. (U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis)

About the site

What is Rich With Reason?

Rich With Reason is a free personal finance education website founded by Jordan Noel, a Temple University finance graduate who works in private equity accounting. It explains money in plain English across personal finance, banking and credit, investing, insurance and financial freedom, with a daily Buzzword of the Day, free money calculators and The Weekly Reason, a free Sunday newsletter.

Every figure on the site is checked against its primary source. The content is educational only and is not financial advice.
