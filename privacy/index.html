Privacy Policy

What information Rich With Reason collects, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Rich With Reason (richwithreason.com) is owned and operated by Jordan Noel ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you visit the site, subscribe to The Weekly Reason or email us, and how we use it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter signups. When you subscribe, we collect your email address and, if you choose to give them, your first name and the topics you're interested in.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything else you choose to include.
  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics (loaded through Google Tag Manager) to understand how the site is used. It uses cookies and similar technology to collect information such as the pages you view, how you arrived at the site, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or region level). Google processes your IP address to provide this service.
  • Newsletter activity. Like most email newsletters, The Weekly Reason is sent through Wix, which can record whether an email was opened and which links were clicked. We use this only to see which topics are useful.
  • Photos. Article photos load from Unsplash (images.unsplash.com), which receives your IP address and basic browser details when an image loads, like any website you visit.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log your IP address and basic request details for security and to keep the site running.
  • Browser storage. To send your newsletter signup, the site stores a short-lived visitor token from our email provider, Wix, in your browser's local storage. It is not used to track you across other sites.

We don't ask for, and you shouldn't send us, account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive financial details.

How we use it

  • To send The Weekly Reason and tailor the topics it covers.
  • To reply to your messages and, with your permission, answer your question in a guide.
  • To see which guides are useful, fix problems and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and comply with the law.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't run ads on the site. We use a small number of service providers who process information for us:

  • Wix, which stores newsletter subscribers and sends The Weekly Reason.
  • Google, for Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, the fonts used on the site (Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts load) and our Gmail inbox.
  • GitHub, which hosts the site.
  • Unsplash, which serves the photos on our articles.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from The Weekly Reason at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Access, correct or delete your information by emailing richwreason@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and within any deadline the law in your state sets.
  • Analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Do Not Track. Browsers handle Do Not Track signals inconsistently, so the site doesn't currently respond to them. The choices above still apply.

State privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information, but you can use any of these rights by emailing us. We won't treat you differently for doing so.

How long we keep it

We keep subscriber information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep emails as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the conversation. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in our account, after which Google deletes it.

Children

Rich With Reason is written for adults and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no website or email service is completely secure.

Visitors outside the United States

Rich With Reason is run from the United States, and information is processed there by us and our providers.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email richwreason@gmail.com.
