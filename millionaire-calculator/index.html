How this calculator works

It starts with what you've already invested, adds your monthly investment, and grows the total each month at the average return you choose. Then it tells you when the balance would first pass $1 million, and how much of that came from your own money versus growth.

A reality check on inflation

$1 million in 30 years won't buy what $1 million buys today. With 3% yearly inflation, it would feel more like about $412,000 in today's money. That's still a lot, but it's why many people set their target from their expected spending instead. Our guide to your financial independence number shows how.

How to get there sooner

  1. Invest more each month. Grab your full employer 401(k) match first, it's free money.
  2. Raise your contribution with every raise. You won't miss money you never got used to spending.
  3. Keep costs low. Broad, low-cost index funds keep more of your return working for you. See why expense ratios matter.
  4. Stay invested. Selling during downturns is one of the most common ways people fall behind.

Common questions

How much do I need to invest a month to become a millionaire?

It depends on how long you have. Starting from $0 at a 7% average return, you'd need about $380 a month for 40 years, about $820 a month for 30 years or about $1,920 a month for 20 years.

How long does it take to become a millionaire investing $1,000 a month?

Starting from $0 at a 7% average annual return, about 27 and a half years. At 10%, it takes about 22 and a half years. Real returns vary year to year.

Is $1 million enough to retire?

Using the 4% rule as a rough guide, $1 million could support about $40,000 a year of withdrawals, adjusted for inflation, plus any Social Security or pension income. Whether that's enough depends on your spending.

Does this calculator account for inflation?

No. It shows dollar amounts in the future. Because prices rise over time, $1 million then will buy less than $1 million today. To get a rough feel in today's money, lower the return you enter by about 2 to 3 points.
