Why renters insurance is the best deal in finance

For about the cost of a streaming subscription, renters insurance protects your belongings, covers you if someone gets hurt and pays for a hotel if your home becomes unlivable.

By the Rich With Reason teamNov 17, 20265 min read
The short version
  • Renters insurance typically costs about $13 to $23 a month in 2026, depending on the study and where you live.
  • It covers your belongings, your liability if someone is hurt and extra living costs if you're displaced.
  • Your landlord's insurance covers the building, not your stuff and not you.

Many renters skip insurance because they assume they don't own enough to protect, or that their landlord's policy covers them. Both are usually wrong. For a small monthly cost, renters insurance offers some of the best value for money in personal finance.

What renters insurance covers

1. Your personal property

Your belongings are covered against risks like fire, smoke, theft, vandalism, certain water damage (such as a burst pipe) and windstorms. Coverage usually follows your belongings, too, so a laptop stolen from your car or a bag stolen while traveling may be covered.

2. Personal liability

If a guest slips in your apartment, or you accidentally cause damage to someone else's property (say your overflowing bathtub floods the unit below), liability coverage helps pay for their medical bills, repairs and legal costs. Policies often include $100,000 or more. This may be the most valuable part of the whole policy.

3. Additional living expenses

If a covered event like a fire makes your home unlivable, this helps pay for a hotel, restaurant meals and other extra costs while repairs happen.

4. Medical payments to others

Small medical bills for guests injured in your home, regardless of who was at fault.

What it usually doesn't cover

  • Floods and earthquakes. These require separate policies.
  • Your roommate's belongings, unless they're named on your policy.
  • High-value items above certain limits, like jewelry, watches or collectibles. Add a "rider" (extra coverage) for these.
  • Pest damage and general wear and tear.
  • Your car. Auto insurance covers the car, though belongings stolen from it may be covered by renters insurance.

Why it's such a good deal

Think about what it would cost to replace everything you own: clothes, furniture, electronics, kitchen gear, bedding. For most people, that adds up to $20,000 or more, far more than they'd guess. Now add potential liability for an injury lawsuit. Renters insurance protects against all of that for roughly the price of a couple of coffees a month.

Quick test: Walk through your home and estimate what it would cost to replace everything if it disappeared tomorrow. Could you afford it out of savings? If not, renters insurance is worth it.

Key choices when buying

Replacement cost vs. actual cash value

Actual cash value pays what your items are worth today, after depreciation, so a five-year-old laptop might get you very little. Replacement cost pays what it costs to buy a new equivalent. Replacement cost costs a bit more, and it's usually worth it.

Coverage amount

Make a quick home inventory (photos or a video of each room work) to estimate your belongings' value. Many renters need $20,000 to $50,000 of property coverage.

Deductible

This is what you pay before insurance kicks in, often $250 to $1,000. A higher deductible lowers your premium, but make sure you could cover it from savings.

How to save on renters insurance

  • Bundle it with car insurance. Multi-policy discounts are common and can make renters insurance nearly free.
  • Ask about safety discounts for smoke detectors, deadbolts and security systems.
  • Pay annually instead of monthly if there's a discount.
  • Compare a few quotes. Prices vary by insurer and location.

Filing a claim: be prepared

  1. Keep a home inventory: photos, a video walkthrough and receipts for big purchases.
  2. Store it in the cloud, not just on a device that could be lost in the same event.
  3. After a loss, document the damage, file a police report if anything was stolen, and contact your insurer quickly.

The bottom line

Renters insurance protects everything you own, shields you from lawsuits and gives you a place to stay if disaster strikes, all for a small monthly cost. If you rent and don't have it, getting a quote takes about 10 minutes.

Average costs are 2026 estimates from several rate studies and vary by location and coverage. Coverage details vary by policy, so read yours carefully. This article is for education only and isn't insurance advice.
