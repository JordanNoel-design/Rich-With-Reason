Disclaimer and Disclosures

What Rich With Reason is, what it isn't, and how it's paid for.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

For education only

Rich With Reason explains how money works in plain English. Everything on the site and in The Weekly Reason is general information for educational purposes. It is not personalized financial, investment, tax, insurance, accounting or legal advice, and nothing here is an offer or recommendation to buy or sell any security, insurance policy or other product. Rich With Reason is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, insurance agency or tax preparer. Your situation is unique, so consider speaking with a qualified, licensed professional before making decisions.

Investing involves risk

All investing involves risk, including the possible loss of the money you invest. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results. Examples that use an average return, such as 7% a year, are hypothetical illustrations, not predictions or promises.

Figures, rates and calculators

Rates, contribution limits, fees and other figures are accurate to the best of our knowledge as of the date shown on each guide, and they change often. Calculator results are estimates based on simplified assumptions. Always confirm current terms with the bank, lender, insurer or official source.

How Rich With Reason makes money

Right now, Rich With Reason doesn't earn money from affiliate links, ads, sponsored posts or paid placements, and no company pays us to mention or rank its products. When we name a bank, fund, card or app, it's to give a real-world example, not an endorsement.

If that ever changes, we'll follow the Federal Trade Commission's endorsement guidelines: affiliate links and sponsored content will be clearly labeled on the page where they appear, and compensation will never change what we say about a product.

Views are the author's own

Rich With Reason is written by Jordan Noel. Opinions on the site are the author's own and don't represent the views of any employer or other organization.

News and sources

The Buzzword of the Day, Buzzword Briefs and trending posts explain money news in our own words and link to the original reporting or official source so you can read more. Headlines, articles and trademarks of news organizations and companies belong to their owners. We don't republish their articles.
