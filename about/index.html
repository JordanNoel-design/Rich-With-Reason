About Rich With Reason

Finance, made digestible for everyone.

No jargon, no hype, no assumption that you already know the lingo. Just clear explanations of how money works, so you can make your next decision with confidence.

Hi, I'm Jordan Noel.

I studied finance at Temple University, and today I work in private equity accounting, keeping track of where investment dollars go and how they grow.

Outside the office, I run Rich With Reason, where I turn the finance-speak I hear every day into plain English you can actually use.

Working in finance taught me something that surprised me: most money topics aren't actually hard. They just tend to be explained in jargon, by people who forget what it was like not to know the terms. That gap keeps a lot of smart people from making confident decisions about their own money.

Rich With Reason is my attempt to close it. My goal is simple: make finance digestible to anyone. Whether you're opening your first savings account, trying to figure out what a "rate hike" means for you, or finally getting serious about investing, you should be able to read a guide here and walk away understanding it.

What you'll find here

  • Plain-English guides on banking and credit, personal finance, investing, insurance and financial freedom.
  • The Buzzword Brief: the finance terms everyone's using, decoded in a couple of minutes.
  • The Weekly Reason: one short email every Sunday with a money term, a take on the week's news and one small action.
